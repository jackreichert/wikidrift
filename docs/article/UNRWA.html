Do the languages agree on basic facts?

Simple factual questions checked in several languages. Editions may agree, differ, contradict, or not say enough to tell. That is not a claim about who is right.

On the latest check, 3 questions produced: 0 contradict · 1 compatible difference · 2 agree · 0 not enough said.

questiontoday
In what year was UNRWA established, and by which body?agree
All editions agree: 1949, UN General Assembly, Resolution 302.
How does UNRWA define a 'Palestine refugee', and does that definition include descendants?differ
en and he give consistent definitions including descendants (he adds paternal line/adoption detail); ar states no definition given, which is insufficient rather than contradictory.
Which populations and territories does UNRWA's mandate cover?agree
All three list the same five areas: Jordan, Lebanon, Syria, West Bank (incl. East Jerusalem), Gaza Strip.
