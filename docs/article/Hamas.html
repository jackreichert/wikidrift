Cross-language lead comparison

How the opening of the article presents the topic in different languages. Disagreement is a reason to read carefully, not a final answer.

Across this narrow window (surrounding the October 7, 2023 attack), the English lead remained textually unchanged between the BEFORE and AFTER revisions. Other editions showed brief, mostly transient edits: Arabic saw a vandalism-like insertion predicting Hamas's disappearance, later presumably reverted, which sits outside and contradicts the stable English framing. French added a substantive historical claim about Israeli support for Hamas's early development to weaken the PLO, a detail absent from English. Hebrew and Russian showed no substantive textual change in this window. A persistent cross-edition divergence unrelated to the time window is the framing of Hamas as 'terrorist' in English versus more neutral 'resistance movement' framing in Arabic, reflecting differing national/political perspectives rather than temporal drift within this snapshot.
