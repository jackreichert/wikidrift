Where the openings part ways

How the opening of the article presents the topic in different languages. Disagreement is a reason to read carefully, not a final answer.

No genuine temporal divergence was found: the English lead text is byte-identical between the BEFORE and AFTER revisions in this window, and the same holds for Arabic, Hebrew, Japanese, and Urdu editions (only a stray link was added in Hebrew, with no textual change). Thus any cross-edition differences identified—such as the AR/HE/JA/UR articles' differing framing of Zionism's origins, goals regarding Palestinian Arabs, characterization of 1948 events, and treatment of anti-Zionism—are static, pre-existing divergences rather than changes driven by the English rewrite. These reflect long-standing editorial differences in emphasis (e.g., Urdu and Arabic framing Zionism more critically via colonialism/ethnic cleansing language, while English's stable phrasing on 'as few Palestinian Arabs as possible' has no equivalent in the other editions) that persisted unchanged across the compared revisions.
