Cross-language lead comparison

How the opening of the article presents the topic in different languages. Disagreement is a reason to read carefully, not a final answer.

The ES lead is a truncated stub covering only the opening 19th-century context about Zionist founders like Max Nordau reframing Jewishness in racial terms. It omits nearly all subsequent content present in the EN lead, including the assimilationist counter-view, internal Jewish debates over diaspora life, the 1930s German Jewish response to Nazism, the Holocaust's discrediting of race science, the founding of Israel and the Law of Return, and modern controversies over population genetics and Zionist narratives. These are omissions due to article incompleteness rather than direct contradictions, but they represent substantial informational gaps for a researcher comparing editions.
