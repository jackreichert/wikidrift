Cross-language lead comparison

How the opening of the article presents the topic in different languages. Disagreement is a reason to read carefully, not a final answer.

The EN Wikipedia lead is the most detailed, including specific paleontological attribution (Tschopp et al.), precise geographic and stratigraphic data, detailed size estimates per species, and taxonomic classification within Diplodocidae/Apatosaurinae. The IT edition attributes the 2015 reclassification study to an England-Portugal research group rather than naming Tschopp, and adds historical detail about the 1903 reclassification not found in EN. The MK edition (largely garbled with template code) offers a more generalized, less precise account, omitting specific dates, locations narrowed to Utah/Wyoming, and detailed taxonomic subfamily discussion, while giving broader size and geographic claims lacking species-level distinction found in EN.
