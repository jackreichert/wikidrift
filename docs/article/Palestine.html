Cross-language lead comparison

How the opening of the article presents the topic in different languages. Disagreement is a reason to read carefully, not a final answer.

Across this narrow window, the English lead remained textually unchanged, so no genuine temporal shift occurred within EN itself. However, comparing EN to other editions reveals persistent and, in one case, newly widened divergences: French shifted during this window from calling Palestine a 'non-member observer state' to explicitly downgrading it to a 'proto-État,' moving further from English's 'partially recognized de jure sovereign state' framing. Hebrew's edit added the loaded term 'terror organizations' to describe Hamas and Islamic Jihad, a framing absent from English. Arabic's description of Palestine as a non-independent, aspirational entity contradicts English's sovereign-state framing, and this contradiction persisted unchanged across the window. Greek showed no substantive lead changes in this period.“
