The durable spineThe more persistent half of the wording present at the start of a candidate window. is the more persistent half of the wording present at the start of a candidate interval. The drop is the percentage-point decline in how much of that wording survives across the whole candidate window. The linked before-and-after revision pair locates the dominant step within that window; the percentage still measures the whole window. It measures established wording loss, not whether the resulting text is better, worse, more neutral, or less neutral.

Cross-language lead comparison

How the opening of the article presents the topic in different languages. Disagreement is a reason to read carefully, not a final answer.

The English lead saw minor wording changes (dropping 'contemporary' and 'political' as modifiers, rephrasing the Trump sentence, and adding new economic policy content on taxes and deregulation), reflecting incremental editorial refinement rather than a shift in substantive claims. The German edition, in contrast, underwent a substantive and evaluative change, altering neutral descriptive language ('die kleinere') to charged terms ('die gestörtere' and 'plutokratischen'), which diverges sharply from the English framing during the same window. The Romanian edition showed no textual changes at all between the two revisions, preserving its earlier characterization of the party unchanged, meaning any earlier or persistent divergences with English remain unaltered by this comparison window.
