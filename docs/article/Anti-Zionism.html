When did anti-Zionism emerge as a distinct position?differ
Editions vary widely between late 19th century, early 20th century, pre-1948, post-1948, or not stated, but these represent varying emphasis/detail rather than strictly incompatible claims, though some (e.g. 1960s Soviet turn, 1948 creation) diverge substantially from 'as old as Zionism' claims.
Which religious and political groups does the article identify as holding anti-Zionist positions?differ
All editions list overlapping categories (Orthodox/Haredi Jews, Bundists, Reform Jews, Arab/Palestinian groups, leftists) but with different emphasis, completeness, and additional groups (e.g. Soviet Communist Party, Catholic Church, white supremacists), reflecting differing detail rather than direct contradiction.
