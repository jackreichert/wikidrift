Do the editions agree on facts?

Whether editions agree on load-bearing facts (counts, categories). Contradictions — especially ones later corrected — are worth checking.

factual questionnow
When did anti-Zionism emerge as a distinct position?differ
Most editions place origins in the late 19th/early 20th century alongside Zionism itself, but several give specific alternative anchors (1897, 1902, 1948, 1967) or leave it unstated, producing varied but largely compatible timelines rather than outright contradiction.
Which religious and political groups does the article identify as holding anti-Zionist positions?differ
All editions converge on a core set (Orthodox/Haredi Jews like Neturei Karta/Satmar, Bundists/leftist Jewish groups, Arab/Palestinian/Muslim groups, American Council for Judaism) but vary widely in additional detail, emphasis, and inclusion of extra groups (e.g., Catholic Church, neo-Nazis, Soviet Communist Party), reflecting differing scope rather than factual conflict.
