Where the openings part ways

How the opening of the article presents the topic in different languages. Disagreement is a reason to read carefully, not a final answer.

The English lead text is byte-identical between the before and after revisions in this window, showing no substantive change. The Arabic edition, however, updated its Gaza casualty figures (from ~34,000/19,324 children/9,823 women to 34,622/14,944 children/9,849 women, closer to the English figures) and added explicit causal framing ('in response') linking Israel's declaration of war, blockade, and bombing campaign to the initial Hamas attack, converging its structure toward the English narrative. Spanish, French, and Hebrew leads show no textual changes across their respective before/after snapshots. A persistent contradiction exists between English (1,139 killed, 252 hostages) and Arabic (at least 1,400 killed, 260 at the festival) regarding initial Israeli casualties, unresolved by either edition's edits in this window.
