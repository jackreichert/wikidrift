Cross-language lead comparison

How the opening of the article presents the topic in different languages. Disagreement is a reason to read carefully, not a final answer.

The English, Hungarian, and Armenian leads closely mirror each other, framing the Nakba unambiguously as ethnic cleansing with specific figures (750,000 expelled, 500+ towns depopulated, 78% land control, biological warfare via well poisoning). The Hebrew edition diverges substantially, presenting the causes as a contested historiographical debate between Zionist, Palestinian, and New Historian perspectives, using a range of refugee estimates, and omitting claims like biological warfare while adding details like the 2023 UN commemoration. The Arabic edition uses starker language ('ethnic cleansing war'), includes additional details absent from EN such as Arafat's 1998 formalization of Nakba Day, Bedouin expulsion from the Negev, and Joseph Massad's argument that the Nakba's true origin dates to 1881, framing it as an ongoing colonial process rather than a bounded historical event.
