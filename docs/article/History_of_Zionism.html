What is a durable-spine dropThe percentage-point decline in durable-spine survival across the whole candidate window.?

The durable spineThe more persistent half of the wording present at the start of a candidate window. is the more persistent half of the wording present at the start of a candidate interval. The drop is the percentage-point decline in how much of that wording survives across the whole candidate window. The linked before-and-after revision pair locates the dominant step within that window; the percentage still measures the whole window. It measures established wording loss, not whether the resulting text is better, worse, more neutral, or less neutral.

Do the languages agree on basic facts?

Simple factual questions checked in several languages. Editions may agree, differ, contradict, or not say enough to tell. That is not a claim about who is right.

On the latest check, 3 questions produced: 0 contradict · 0 compatible difference · 2 agree · 1 not enough said.

questiontoday
In what year and city was the First Zionist Congress held, and who convened it?not enough said
Most editions state nothing specific about the congress; only ur (and partially uk) give Basel 1897 with Herzl, others only mention 1897 founding without city/congress detail.
Approximately when did the modern political Zionist movement emerge?agree
All editions converge on 1897 or late 19th century, consistent framing of the same period.
In what geographic region did the movement seek to establish a Jewish homeland?agree
All editions agree the goal was Palestine/Land of Israel, with en adding specific geographic boundaries.
