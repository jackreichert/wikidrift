All editions affirm shared Middle Eastern/Levantine ancestry among major Jewish groups, with some noting host-population admixture.

Editions agree on Middle Eastern majority plus Southern European admixture, but numeric splits differ sharply: ar/he cite ~70/30, pt cites 40-50% Levantine vs 50-60% European (reversing the majority), a large numeric contradiction.
