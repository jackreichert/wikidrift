Cross-language lead comparison

How the opening of the article presents the topic in different languages. Disagreement is a reason to read carefully, not a final answer.

The three editions largely agree on the core findings (shared Middle Eastern ancestry, genetic isolates, Ashkenazi as largest group, Southern European admixture), but diverge in framing and specific details. MK and AR situate the topic more explicitly within population genetics methodology tied to historical/linguistic research, which EN omits. MK adds specific named comparator populations (Lebanese, Palestinians, Bedouins, Druze) not found in EN. AR attributes specific causal historical claims to Behar et al. connecting genetics to biblical Israelite descent, a framing absent in EN. Most notably, EN and MK hedge on the ancestry of Bene Israel, Cochin Jews, and Beta Israel ('may have some admixture'), while AR asserts more definitively that they descend from ancient Jewish origins, a subtle but meaningful contradiction in certainty of claims.
