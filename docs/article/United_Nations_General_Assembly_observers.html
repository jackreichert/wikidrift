Cross-language lead comparison

How the opening of the article presents the topic in different languages. Disagreement is a reason to read carefully, not a final answer.

The three editions are largely aligned in structure and core content regarding UNGA observer status, the 1986 treaty conference, and the EU's exceptional 2011 rights grant. The main divergences are: EN uniquely names the two specific non-member observer states (Holy See and State of Palestine), which KO and FR leave unspecified; FR adds more granularity by distinguishing intergovernmental from non-governmental organizations among non-state observers, a detail absent in EN and KO; and there is a minor difference in whether the 'as of May 2011' temporal qualifier is retained when describing the EU's uniqueness, with FR omitting it while EN and KO include it.
