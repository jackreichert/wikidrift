Cross-language lead comparison

How the opening of the article presents the topic in different languages. Disagreement is a reason to read carefully, not a final answer.

The three editions largely agree on core facts (rules, pieces, objective, FIDE, Steinitz as first champion, Gukesh as current champion) but diverge notably on historical framing: EN and FR disagree on the century and route by which chess reached Europe and on how far back organized competition dates (FR citing 8th-century Baghdad and an 1851 London tournament, EN citing only the 19th century). UK gives a distinctly different account of chess's European arrival (9th century via Umayyad Spain) and omits the Deep Blue/Kasparov narrative entirely, while adding extensive detail on time controls (blitz, rapid, bullet) absent from EN and FR. FR is the most concise on computer chess, lacking the detailed history EN provides.
