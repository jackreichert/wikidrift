Cross-language lead comparison

How the opening of the article presents the topic in different languages. Disagreement is a reason to read carefully, not a final answer.

The editions diverge significantly in framing and scope. EN presents a detailed, statistics-heavy account centered on 'political violence'/insurgency with nuanced terrorism designations, named groups, and tactics. AR reframes the entire topic as legitimate 'resistance' (المقاومة) spanning back to 1918, avoiding the term terrorism entirely. HE and FR define the topic outright as 'terrorism' without EN's qualifications about differing international designations. SIMPLE presents a more absolutist goal (disestablishing Israel) compared to EN's more measured framing around ending occupation and self-determination. Statistical data and named organizations present in EN are largely absent from the shorter other-language leads.
