Cross-language lead comparison

How the opening of the article presents the topic in different languages. Disagreement is a reason to read carefully, not a final answer.

The editions largely agree on Omar's basic biography, political positions, and status as a trailblazing Somali-American, Muslim congresswoman, but diverge in framing of her identity milestones (e.g., 'woman of color' vs 'black woman' vs 'non-white woman'; naturalized citizen framing), in inclusion of controversy over her naturalization narrative (EL only), in mention of Trump's travel ban opposition (EL only), in explicit description of Trump's actions as 'propaganda' (UR) versus 'derogatory comments' (EN), and in the EN-exclusive mention of her 2023 removal from the Foreign Affairs Committee, which is absent from both EL and UR leads.
