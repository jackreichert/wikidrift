Cross-language lead comparison

How the opening of the article presents the topic in different languages. Disagreement is a reason to read carefully, not a final answer.

The English lead text is identical between the BEFORE and AFTER revisions in this short window (2018-05-12 to 2018-05-15), showing no substantive change. The Gujarati article also shows no textual change between its BEFORE (2017) and AFTER (2018) revisions, remaining anchored to older IPCC Fourth Assessment Report content (2005/2007 statements) rather than the Fifth Assessment Report cited in English, so the divergence between English and Gujarani content persisted across the window rather than changing. The Chinese entry is merely a redirect in both revisions, but the redirect target itself changed from 'climate change' (氣候變遷) to 'climate variability and change' (氣候變率與變化), a structural/title change with no comparable content to assess against English's stable lead. No genuine convergence or divergence in substantive content can be established from these snapshots beyond the persistence of the Gujarati/English gap and the Chinese redirect retargeting.
