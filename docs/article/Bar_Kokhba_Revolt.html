Cross-language lead comparison

How the opening of the article presents the topic in different languages. Disagreement is a reason to read carefully, not a final answer.

Across this window, the EN, AR, DE, and HE lead texts were unchanged verbatim (no textual edits within the compared revisions), while FA underwent a substantial rewrite that added several paragraphs closely paralleling EN's structure (resentment after 70 AD, Aelia Capitolina, circumcision ban, Betar, consequences) while retaining its older paragraph about mass killings and expulsion. No genuine new divergence was introduced by EN itself since its text is identical before/after; the flagged differences are long-standing cross-edition differences (e.g., casualty figures, unmentioned religious prohibitions, Christianity-Judaism divide) that persisted through this period rather than emerging from an EN edit. FA's revision represents convergence toward the EN framing without resolving all divergences.
