Cross-language lead comparison

How the opening of the article presents the topic in different languages. Disagreement is a reason to read carefully, not a final answer.

The EN and TR (and BN, which closely mirrors EN) leads diverge mainly in scope and emphasis. TR omits EN's detailed discussion of criticisms of the 1948 Genocide Convention's definition, while EN omits TR's citations of Patrick Wolfe and David Maybury-Lewis and TR's specific modern examples of alleged cultural genocide (Uyghurs, Tibetans, Native Americans, First Nations). The editions also differ slightly on which continents/regions are cited as sites of colonial genocidal violence, and TR presents a more direct quote attributing to Lemkin the view that colonization was 'essentially genocide,' whereas EN frames this more abstractly as a two-stage process. BN is essentially a direct translation of EN with no substantive divergences found.
