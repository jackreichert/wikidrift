Cross-language lead comparison

How the opening of the article presents the topic in different languages. Disagreement is a reason to read carefully, not a final answer.

In this narrow time window, the English lead text remained textually identical before and after (only revision metadata differs), so no genuine English-side change occurred. The Arabic and Tamil leads also remained essentially unchanged in substance, with Arabic only adding a trailing image caption ('الماء'). Since English did not change, no temporal divergence (moving away, converging, or parallel change) can be established; the only persistent difference is the pre-existing gap in detail and figures between English (highly quantitative, ~1,460 Tt, 71% coverage, aquifer/vapor percentages) versus the much shorter Arabic and Tamil descriptions (basic definition, 70% coverage, no numeric detail), which predates and persists through this window.
