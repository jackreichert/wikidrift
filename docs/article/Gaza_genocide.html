Cross-language lead comparison

How the opening of the article presents the topic in different languages. Disagreement is a reason to read carefully, not a final answer.

Both editions moved from an 'accusation' framing to a declarative genocide framing, showing parallel change. However, they now diverge sharply on core facts: English reports a Gaza Health Ministry toll of 66,148 by October 2025, while Arabic claims over 80,000 killed by the same date—a direct numeric contradiction. Healthcare worker deaths also diverge (Arabic cites 723 by Oct 2025; English's equivalent figure is absent, replaced by a health-centre destruction statistic). The ICJ ruling description diverges: English now omits the 'plausible intent' language present in Arabic, describing measures ordered instead. English has also expanded substantially with new content absent from Arabic—institutional genocide recognition and detailed famine/starvation data—reflecting English's greater elaboration during this window while Arabic retained more of the original 2024 structure with only date/number updates.
