Where the openings part ways

How the opening of the article presents the topic in different languages. Disagreement is a reason to read carefully, not a final answer.

The EN lead emphasizes marginality of collaboration as a point of national pride and frames punishment by the government-in-exile and resistance. The RU lead is more analytical, providing Madajczyk's specific 5% estimate compared to 25% resistance, categorizing collaboration into industrial/political/military/cultural forms, and introducing John Connelly's critique of 'structural collaboration' regarding indifference to Jewish suffering—none of which appear in the EN lead. The AR lead closely mirrors EN but restructures the opening to foreground Poland's Allied membership and omits the 'point of pride' framing. Overall, RU diverges most significantly by adding analytical depth and a critical perspective absent from EN and AR leads.
