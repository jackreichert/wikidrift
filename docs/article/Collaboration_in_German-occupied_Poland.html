Cross-language lead comparison

How the opening of the article presents the topic in different languages. Disagreement is a reason to read carefully, not a final answer.

The English lead was substantially revised between June 2018 and January 2019, removing the specific claim that the German minority were the main collaborators, softening 'executed' to 'punished... with thousands sentenced to death,' and rephrasing the comparative claim about marginality using hedged attribution ('described by some'). The Russian lead's brief definitional lead remained completely unchanged across the same window, containing no equivalent statements about collaborator identity, punishment specifics, or comparative marginality, making these purely English-side edits with no cross-edition convergence or divergence pattern in RU to compare against.

Compared matched historical revisions around the L1 candidate window from 2018-07-01 to 2019-01-01.
