Cross-language lead comparison

How the opening of the article presents the topic in different languages. Disagreement is a reason to read carefully, not a final answer.

The editions diverge significantly in framing, scope, and factual emphasis. EN presents a military-operation narrative with a timeline including the 2025 ceasefire and resumption of hostilities. AR emphasizes Israel's rejection of Hamas-accepted ceasefire proposals, differing from EN's statement that negotiations failed. HE frames the event as a tactical battle with detailed IDF unit compositions and casualty and tunnel-destruction claims absent from EN. DE includes international legal context and hostage-related motivations absent from EN. BN describes deliberate ethnic cleansing, destruction in Rafah, and global political reactions not found in the other selected leads. These are differences for further inspection, not a judgment about which edition is correct.
