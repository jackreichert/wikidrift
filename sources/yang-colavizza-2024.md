# Polarization and reliability of news sources in Wikipedia

| | |
|---|---|
| **Authors** | Puyu Yang, Giovanni Colavizza |
| **Venue** | *Online Information Review* 48(5), 2024 |
| **DOI** | https://doi.org/10.1108/OIR-02-2023-0084 |
| **arXiv** | https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.16065 |

## What it is

Large-scale study of **which news domains Wikipedia cites** (~30M citations on English Wikipedia), scored for political polarization and reliability.

## Findings

- Moderate but systematic **liberal** polarization in news-media source selection.
- News is a large share of Wikipedia citations (~one third).
- Reliability / polarization patterns can be measured from citation graphs without reading full article stance.

## Relation to WikiDrift

Prior art for **citation-source analysis** as a bias-adjacent signal. WikiDrift L5 #3b is **temporal and article-local**: composition change *across an L1 pivot*, domains added/dropped, **no** reliability/advocacy ratings attached. Yang–Colavizza show the cross-sectional global pattern; WikiDrift shows per-article from→to at rewrite time.
