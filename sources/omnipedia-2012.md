## What it is

Interactive system for reading **up to 25 language editions** of Wikipedia at once: extract and align concepts so readers see what each language community covers or omits.

## Method (sketch)

- Align articles across languages (same topic).
- Topic / concept extraction and visualization of multi-edition coverage.
- HCI evaluation of multilingual reading.

## Findings / contribution

Language editions are **not translations** of one global text; they encode different “linguistic points of view.” Multilingual UI makes that gap visible.

## Relation to WikiDrift

**Ancestor of L5 instrument #1 (cross-lingual framing).** Omnipedia is snapshot / coverage-oriented. WikiDrift adds:

- Stance classification per edition (not only concept presence).
- **Pivot-relative** comparison (did English peel from others *at* the L1 pivot?).
- Receipts (oldids) for verification.
