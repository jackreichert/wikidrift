# Recommended Practices for NPOV Research on Wikipedia

| | |
|---|---|
| **Authors** | Isaac Johnson, Yu-Ming Liou, Jacob Rogers, Aaron Shaw, Leila Zia |
| **Year** | 2025 |
| **arXiv** | https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.21526 |

## What it is

Guidance paper from Wikimedia Research–adjacent authors: how researchers should study **Neutral Point of View** so results are valid and useful to Wikipedia communities.

## Core arguments

- NPOV is a **Wikipedia-specific** policy concept — not the same as “no bias,” “both sides,” or sentiment polarity.
- Neutrality research is under-produced relative to its importance, partly because NPOV is hard and contested.
- Common failure modes: using **sentiment** as a stand-in for viewpoint balance (tone ≠ NPOV), ignoring community definitions, poor communication of results for on-wiki impact.

## Relation to WikiDrift

**Hard constraint on L2.** Stance classifier is explicitly on an **encyclopedic neutrality axis** (critical / neutral / sympathetic toward focal entities + NPOV-departure), not VADER-style sentiment. Johnson is the cited reason the 006 VADER prototype was demoted and 010 LLM stance became production.
