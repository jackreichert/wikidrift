## What it is

Long scholarly essay arguing that **English Wikipedia** has systematically distorted Holocaust history — especially regarding **Polish society’s role** — via biased framing, source selection, and editing patterns over many years (not classic “denial,” but distortion/minimization).

## Method (sketch)

Historical close reading + edit-history investigation across many articles and related talk/user activity (manual scholarly method, not an automated detector).

## Findings (high level)

- Multi-year patterns of whitewashing Polish complicity and recycling tropes about Jews.
- Some distortions **stood for years** (including cases later used as public examples).
- Sparked major public and on-wiki response (including topic bans for some editors).

## Relation to WikiDrift

**Primary motivation for the Holocaust-in-Poland slate and the L5 gap.**

- *Warsaw concentration camp* (KL Warschau myth) is the textbook **born/stable distortion**: L1/L2 may miss it; cross-edition **fact** check catches numerical myths.
- Benchmark roster labels several Poland/Holocaust articles with Grabowski–Klein as a source of adjudicated concern (articles as analytical objects; cases sanction *patterns*, not automated guilt of named accounts in our tool).

Does **not** supply WikiDrift’s detection method — supplies **grounded cases** and the argument that long-standing distortion is real and consequential.
